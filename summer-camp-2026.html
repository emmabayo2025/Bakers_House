Special feature

Robotics Training with TU STEM

What if your daughter left camp not just on fire for God — but equipped with real engineering skills? This summer, she gets both.

  • 🤖

    Hands-on robotics sessionsLed by educators from the TU Center for STEM Excellence, girls will build and program robots using real engineering tools and methods.

  • ⚙️

    No experience neededSessions are designed for beginners — curiosity is the only prerequisite. Every girl can engage, regardless of background.

  • 🔬

    University-backed curriculumTUCSE serves Maryland's K–12 schools with high-quality STEM programming aligned to state and national standards.

  • ✨

    Faith & innovation togetherWe believe God calls women to lead in every field. Robotics training is one more way we equip girls to change the world.
