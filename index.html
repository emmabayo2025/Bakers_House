Who We Are

WE DON'T FOLLOW THE WORLD.
WE SET THE PACE.
WE ARE THE FUTURE.

Baker's House is a youth-centered initiative dedicated to supporting the growth, confidence, and future aspirations of girls, particularly immigrant girls and girls of color. Through mentorship and creative learning, we foster a safe environment where girls build secure foundations.

Grounded in Christian values, our focus is simple: **Word, Worship, and the Holy Spirit.** We equip girls to maximize their potential, grow in leadership, and live out bold, Gospel-focused lives at home and at school.

Learn More

Our Vision & Mandate

OUR VISION

To see every girl — regardless of where she was born, the color of her skin, or the circumstances she came from — rise into her full potential: confident, equipped, and unshakeable.

OUR MANDATE

Baker's House exists to walk alongside girls, particularly Black immigrant girls and girls of color, through the seasons of growing up that can feel isolating, confusing, and overlooked.

We provide a safe, nurturing space where girls are seen, celebrated, and challenged to become the best version of themselves — grounded in Christian values and proud of who they are.

We do this through:

Mentorship

Connecting girls with women who look like them and have walked the road ahead.

Life Skills Training

Practical tools for navigating school, relationships, finances, and the future.

Career Pathways

Exposing girls to fields where women, especially women of color, are underrepresented.

Health & Wellness

Age-appropriate sexual health education and social-emotional learning.

Creative Learning

Experiences that spark curiosity, self-expression, and joy.

Leadership Development

Building the next generation of bold, servant-hearted leaders.

OUR VALUES

Compassion · Discipline · Integrity · Service · Leadership

We honor every girl's cultural identity while rooting her in values that will carry her through life.

Our Mentors

MEET OUR
FOUNDER

Empowering the Next Generation

Dr. Tosin Adebayo knows firsthand what it means to navigate growing up far from home. As a Nigerian immigrant and educator, she understands the unique challenges girls of color face — the longing to belong, the silence of being unseen, and the strength it takes to keep going. Moved by the Holy Spirit and shaped by her own journey, Dr. Adebayo founded Baker's House with one conviction: every girl deserves a safe place where she is known, loved, and equipped to thrive. Her life's work is making sure no girl walks that road alone.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is Baker's House only for girls?

Yes. This is intentionally a girls-only ministry environment so our teaching, groups, and practical mentorship can speak directly to young women following Christ.

What ages do you serve?

Typically middle school through high school (ages 11–18). Our small groups are carefully segmented by age and grade so discussions are appropriate and relevant.

Do we need to attend a specific local church?

No. Baker's House is a non-denominational initiative. We welcome girls from all denominational backgrounds, as well as those who are still exploring church and faith.

How do we get started?

Simply fill out the "Get Connected" form below. We will reach out personally to share gathering times, directions, answer questions, and arrange a welcoming host for your daughter's first visit.

How do I register for Summer Camp 2026?

Visit our Summer Camp page at summer-camp-2026.html for full packing details, cost, schedule, and details. Camp takes place June 25–27, 2026, at Sandy Cove Ministries, MD.
