Who We Are

A Safe Place to Be Seen, Known & Equipped

Baker's House is a youth-centered nonprofit dedicated to supporting the growth, confidence, and future aspirations of girls — particularly immigrant girls and girls of color.

Through mentorship, life skills training, and creative learning, we provide an environment where girls build confidence, explore career pathways, and develop leadership skills.

"Every girl deserves a safe place where she is known, loved, and equipped to thrive."

What We Do

Baker's House exists to walk alongside girls, particularly Black immigrant girls and girls of color, through the seasons of growing up that can feel isolating, confusing, and overlooked.

We provide a safe, nurturing space where girls are seen, celebrated, and challenged to become the best version of themselves — grounded in Christian values and proud of who they are.

Meet Our Executive Director

MEET OUR
EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR

Dr. Tosin Adebayo

Executive Director · Nigerian Immigrant · College Professor

Dr. Tosin Adebayo knows firsthand what it means to navigate growing up far from home. As a Nigerian immigrant and a college professor, she understands the unique challenges girls of color face — the longing to belong, the silence of being unseen, and the strength it takes to keep going.

Moved by the Holy Spirit and shaped by her own journey, Dr. Adebayo founded Baker's House with one conviction: every girl deserves a safe place where she is known, loved, and equipped to thrive. Her life's work is making sure no girl walks that road alone.
