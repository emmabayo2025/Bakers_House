FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is Baker's House only for girls?

Yes. This is intentionally a girls-only ministry so teaching, discussion, and community can speak directly to young women following Christ.

What ages do you serve?

Typically middle school through high school. Exact age bands may vary by location — ask when you connect and we'll walk you through everything.

Do we need to attend a specific church?

Policies differ by site. Reach out and we'll explain how Baker's House partners with families and local churches in your area.

How do we get started?

Use the form below or email us. We'll share gathering times, expectations, and how your daughter can visit for her first night.

What is the commitment level?

Baker's House asks for consistency — showing up, respecting the culture, and keeping communication open with parents. There's no perfect girl here; only girls being transformed by Jesus.

How do I register for Summer Camp 2026?

Visit our Summer Camp page at summer-camp-2026.html for full details — location, cost, packing list, and the registration link. Camp is June 25–27 at Sandy Cove, Maryland.
